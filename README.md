## 📱 Features

- **Luminous Mobile-First Design:** Clean light aesthetic with smooth gradients, frosted glass cards, and micro-interactions.
- **Direct Connect:** 1-tap call to `9837044095`, WhatsApp chat integration, and Google Maps turn-by-turn navigation.
- **AI Review Generator:** Dynamic 3-language generator (English, Hinglish, Hindi) with service chips and 1-click Google Reviews redirect.
- **Interactive Service Selector:** Modal selector to explore phone repairs, 3M skins/lamination, certified 2nd hand phones, and premium accessories.
- **Store Gallery & Lightbox:** High-resolution storefront, display, and workshop photo showcase with interactive viewer.
- **Verified Customer Testimonials & FAQs:** Social proof with 5.0 Google ratings and instant answers.
